Overview

Grind @ The Rise is a specialty café known for freshly brewed coffee, made-to-order food, and a welcoming community vibe. They needed a brand website that matched their premium identity and gave customers everything they needed — menu, location, catering, and their loyalty programme.

The Challenge

The café had a strong local following and bold visual brand identity, but no website to represent it online. They needed something as premium and vibrant as the café itself — large typography, rich food photography, and clear information architecture for customers finding them online.

The Solution

  • Bold Visual Design — Large display typography and full-bleed food photography that mirrors the café's physical brand energy.
  • Full Menu Pages — Separate food and drinks pages with imagery for all items including specials.
  • Catering Page — Dedicated enquiry page for corporate and event bookings.
  • Loyalty Programme — Page explaining the loyalty system and how customers can join.
  • Specials Integration — Easily updatable specials section — team posts seasonal items without developer help.
  • SiteGround CDN — Fast load times across all devices.

"Your daily ritual, elevated." — The hero headline captures the brand voice perfectly, setting the tone for the entire customer experience online.
