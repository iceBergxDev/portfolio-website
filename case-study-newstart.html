Overview

Newstart Homes is a South-East Queensland custom home builder with over 30 years of experience, specialising in award-winning new home designs and fixed-price construction across Brisbane and surrounds.

I built their full website to showcase their home designs catalogue, gallery, and unique selling points — converting visitors from organic search into direct enquiries with the team.

The Challenge

Newstart needed a website that could serve two audiences at once: homebuyers researching their first build (who need education and trust signals) and ready buyers comparing specific home designs (who need a browsable catalogue with specifications).

Their previous online presence didn't reflect the quality of their builds or the breadth of their home design range, leading to lost leads to competitors with stronger digital presence.

The Solution

  • Homlisti Theme — Premium real estate and property theme customised for a home builder context, giving a clean and trustworthy visual identity.
  • Home Designs Catalogue — Full browsable catalogue of house plans with filtering by bedrooms, style, and size — making it easy for buyers to find the right design.
  • Gallery — High-quality photography of completed builds, using before/after image comparison sliders (Elementor add-on) to showcase renovation and construction quality.
  • Why Newstart Section — Trust-building page covering fixed-price contracts, structural warranty, award-winning designs, and their 30+ year track record.
  • Lead Capture — Contact forms on every key page with WP Mail SMTP for reliable delivery, driving direct enquiries to the sales team.
  • SEO & Performance — Yoast SEO configured for local South-East Queensland search terms, NitroPack for fast Core Web Vitals, Really Simple SSL for secure browsing.

"Building Lasting Relationships" — the site's design language reflects this ethos: clean, professional, and built to earn trust before a visitor ever picks up the phone.
