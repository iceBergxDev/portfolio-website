Birdhouse Media

Overview

Birdhouse Media is a Perth-based video production agency specialising in corporate video, TVCs, events, real estate, and music content. Their client portfolio spans major brands including Alcoa, APPEA, Mercedes-Benz, Motorola, Vicinity Centres, and Bodhi.

I built their full website from the ground up — transforming a premium ThemeForest finance theme (Stotage) into a bold, media-forward agency site with custom CPTs, an Elementor-powered theme builder, and a SiteGround-native performance stack.

The Challenge

Birdhouse needed a website that could do several things at once: showcase their work as a filterable portfolio, present 8 distinct services clearly, introduce their team with personality, and convert visitors into enquiries — all while loading fast and looking premium.

The existing Stotage theme was built for finance and consulting — not a creative agency. Every visual component needed to be rebuilt to match Birdhouse's dark, cinematic brand identity.

The Solution

I rebuilt the site architecture around three custom post types and a full Elementor theme builder setup:

  • Portfolio CPT — Filterable project showcase with portfolio-category taxonomy. Visitors can filter by industry (Corporate, Events, Real Estate, Music, etc.).
  • Services CPT — 8 services (Filming, Drone, Editing, Animations, Photography, Script Writing, Directing, Voice Over) each with a dedicated page, rendered as a visual carousel on the homepage.
  • Team with Video Profiles — Each team member has an embedded self-introduction video on their card — a distinctive touch that sets the agency apart.
  • Production Workflow Section — A step-by-step carousel (Initial Meeting → Shoot Planning → Filming → Editing → Final Delivery) that sets client expectations before they enquire.
  • Live Google Reviews — Embedded via Widgets for Google Reviews plugin for social proof directly on-site.
  • Elementor Theme Builder — Header, footer, page titles, and mega menu all managed as pxl-template CPT entries with conditional display logic — no hardcoded layouts in PHP.

The theme builder approach means the client can update any layout element from within Elementor — no developer required for structural changes.

8 Service pages — each with dedicated CPT entry and carousel card
3 Custom Post Types — portfolio, service, team with category taxonomies
40+ Custom Elementor widgets in the Stotage theme ecosystem
