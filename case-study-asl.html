Australian Synthetic Lawns —
E-Commerce & Lead Gen Website

WordPress Divi Builder E-Commerce 2024–2025

Overview

Australian Synthetic Lawns is a supplier of premium synthetic grass products serving residential, commercial, and trade customers across Australia. They needed a website that could showcase their product range and convert visitors into enquiries and sales.

The Challenge

The synthetic lawn market in Australia is competitive, with customers comparing products online before purchasing. The client needed a site that communicated quality visually, provided detailed product information, and captured both retail and trade leads efficiently.

The Solution

  • Custom Divi Theme — Built on the Divi Builder with a custom child theme, giving the client full control over page editing without developer involvement.
  • Product Catalogue — Structured product pages for each grass type with specifications (pile height, colour, warranty) and high-quality photography.
  • Quote Request Flow — Integrated lead capture forms designed for both retail customers and trade/commercial enquiries.
  • Mobile-First Design — Fully responsive layout optimised for users browsing on mobile while planning landscaping projects.
  • SEO Setup — On-page SEO configured for Australian synthetic lawn search terms, with local and national targeting.

Results

Australian Synthetic Lawns has a professional lead-gen site reaching retail and trade customers across Australia.
