Overview

Australian Synthetic Lawns is a supplier of premium synthetic grass products serving residential, commercial, and trade customers across Australia. They needed a website that showcased their product range and converted visitors into enquiries and sales.

The Challenge

The synthetic lawn market in Australia is competitive — customers compare products online before purchasing. The client needed a site that communicated quality visually, provided detailed product information, and captured both retail and trade leads efficiently.

The Solution

  • Custom Divi Theme — Built with a custom child theme giving the client full control over page editing without developer involvement.
  • Product Catalogue — Structured product pages for each grass type with specifications (pile height, colour, warranty) and high-quality photography.
  • Quote Request Flow — Integrated lead capture for both retail customers and trade/commercial enquiries.
  • Mobile-First Design — Fully responsive layout optimised for users browsing on mobile while planning landscaping projects.
  • SEO Setup — On-page SEO configured for Australian synthetic lawn search terms, with local and national targeting.
2 Separate quote flows — retail and trade, less friction per segment
100% Full product catalogue with specs and photography live at launch
Self-serve Client manages all content updates independently via Divi
