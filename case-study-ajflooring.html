AJ Flooring —
Award-Finalist Website

WordPress Elementor Pro Gravity Forms 2025

Overview

AJ Flooring is a professional flooring company based in Australia, offering engineered timber, bamboo, laminate, vinyl plank, and carpet solutions for residential and commercial clients.

I built their full website from the ground up — product showcases, lead capture forms, SEO-optimised content, and performance tuning. The site was recognised as a 2025 Business Champion Award Finalist.

The Challenge

AJ Flooring needed to compete online against larger national flooring chains. They required a professional website that showcased their product range clearly, captured quote requests efficiently, and ranked well in local search for flooring-related terms.

The Solution

  • Product Showcase Pages — Dedicated pages for each flooring category (engineered timber, bamboo, laminate, vinyl, carpet) with product imagery and specifications.
  • Lead Capture Forms — Gravity Forms integrated quote request forms on every product page, routing enquiries directly to the sales team.
  • Technical SEO — Rank Math SEO configured with structured data, local business schema, and page-by-page keyword targeting.
  • Performance — NitroPack cache and image optimisation configured for fast Core Web Vitals scores.
  • Astra Theme — Lightweight Astra theme customised to match the brand identity, with a custom child theme for maintainability.

The site's design and business results were strong enough to earn a 2025 Business Champion Award Finalist nomination.
