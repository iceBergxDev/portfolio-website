Overview

AJ Flooring is a professional flooring company based in Australia, offering engineered timber, bamboo, laminate, vinyl plank, and carpet for residential and commercial clients.

I built their website from the ground up — product showcases, lead capture, SEO-optimised content, and performance tuning. The site was recognised as a 2025 Business Champion Award Finalist.

The Challenge

AJ Flooring needed to compete against larger national flooring chains online. They required a professional site that showcased their product range clearly, captured quote requests efficiently, and ranked well in local search for flooring-related terms.

The Solution

  • Services Showcase — Dedicated pages for Custom Timber Flooring, Installation, Sanding & Polishing, Repairs, Cleaning, and Timber Decking — each with a free quote CTA.
  • Lead Capture Forms — Gravity Forms free measure & quote request on every service page, routing enquiries directly to the sales team.
  • Trust Signals — Bona Certified Floor Finish Contractor badge, ATFA membership, and 40+ years of expertise prominently featured on the homepage.
  • Awards Section — Dedicated carousel showcasing ATFA Runner Up 2023, ATFA NSW/ACT Winner 2024, Trades Champions Finalist 2024, and Business Champion Finalist 2025.
  • Technical SEO — Rank Math with structured data, local business schema, and keyword targeting for Sydney Upper North Shore flooring terms.
  • Performance — NitroPack cache and image optimisation for strong Core Web Vitals scores.

The site's design and business results earned the 2025 Australian Small Business Champion Award Finalist nomination — and ATFA NSW/ACT Winner 2024 for Pre-finished Stair.
